# Process section artwork

Each step has its own background and circular photograph. Step 1 uses forest/conversation; step 2 uses support-cover/support-detail; step 3 uses meeting-cover/meeting-detail. Backgrounds are delivered as 960 × 1280 WebP and circular photographs as 512 × 512 WebP. All assets were created with the built-in image_gen tool in generation mode.

## process-support-detail

File: public/images/process/support-detail.webp

Prompt: Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square editorial photograph for a small circular image on a social care website, representing personal support. Close-up of one older adult hand being gently supported by the relaxed hands of another adult, natural believable anatomy, everyday unpolished skin textures, simple oat linen and muted sage knitted sleeves. Only hands and forearms visible, no faces or identifiable people. The hands are centered within the inner 65 percent of a square composition so the full meaningful gesture fits a round crop. Soft warm window daylight, quiet cream background out of focus, premium but honest documentary still-life mood. Earthy olive, warm ivory and skin tones, subtle grain. No writing, logos, furniture setting, mugs, notebook, medical gear, rings, exaggerated emotions, stock-photo handshake, or plastic skin. Full bleed square photo.
